## Non-negotiable headline

> In this transparent **synthetic event**, six hours of actionable warning reaches the
> same **illustrative 1.2 km margin** with one-eighth of the **manoeuvre-budget demand**
> required at 45 minutes.

The result is constructed from the ideal linear relationship `displacement = Δv × lead
time`: six hours is eight times 45 minutes, so `0.40 m/s ÷ 0.05 m/s = 8` for the two
equal-margin options. It is not measured operational performance.

## Non-negotiable public-data boundary

The public picture is a dated CelesTrak snapshot or labelled historical replay. SGP4
drives the bounded four-object catalogue globe; parsed SOCRATES Plus rows drive the
separate OneWeb-filtered radial candidate display. UK Orbit Guard performs no local
pairwise detector. Both views are approximate/incomplete and separate from the synthetic
encounter. Their output is **candidate for further review**, never collision, threat,
safe pass or manoeuvre recommendation.

The CAA-linked example—UK-DMC-2, `2009-041C`, catalogue `35683`—is a civil public
provenance example. It is not presented as a current operational asset, military or RAF
property, a protected service, or an endorsement.
